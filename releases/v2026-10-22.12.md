# Release v2026-10-22.12 — evidence

Impact: additive

## What changed

The adversarial corpus synthesizes its own fixture project instead of mutating
the project's corpus.

## Why

`00-llm-integration` reached full conformance and then failed `make test` at case
one of 54:

```
sed: can't read /tmp/.../prompts/tasks/01-site-build-implement.md: No such file
```

The corpus claimed to validate the validator against *any* project, but it
mutated two files that only a skeleton descendant has: the site-build feature and
its task. Every project that does not model a site build that way — and that is a
legitimate choice — failed all 54 cases at once. The skeleton, elseon and the
canary passed by lineage, not by design.

## The fix

`reset()` writes a canonical feature (`89-site-build.md`, identifiers
`FIXTURE-R###`) and task (`89-site-build-implement.md`) into the fixture every
time, under reserved numbers, and the mutating cases target those. Two
consequences:

- A project's own corpus no longer affects the corpus test at all, so the test
  cannot drift with it.
- Two cases that exploited `02-project-pages.md` depending on a skeleton feature
  now add that dependency to the fixture themselves. A mutation belongs in the
  test, not in a shared file.

## Why the guard missed it

`tools/tests/08-shared-references.sh` exempts `tests/`, because a test writes
paths it synthesizes and those are not references. That exemption is correct in
general and was wrong here: this test hardcoded a project file. The corpus now
contains no project-specific path, and the guard's exemption stands for the right
reason rather than hiding a defect.

## Machine-checkable evidence

- `00-llm-integration`, which has no site-build feature: `10-prompt-validator`
  reports `54 rejected, 6 accepted` — the same counts as the skeleton.
- Library suite: 9 tests.
- Three projects re-vendored after this release, suites green.

## Behavioral evidence

Not applicable: the corpus is the harness, not a rule. What it changes is the
*coverage* of a project that diverged: it now gets the adversarial suite it was
always supposed to have.
